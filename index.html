Travel information

Helpful pointers for planning your journey to India and beyond.

Visas & entry

  • Most nationalities qualify for India's e-Visa — apply at least 4 business days before travel.
  • Check visa requirements for every country on your itinerary; rules change frequently.
  • Always carry printed copies of your e-Visa and hotel confirmations at immigration.

Money & payments

  • India uses INR (Indian Rupee). ATMs are widely available in cities but carry cash in rural areas.
  • UPI and cards are accepted in most hotels and restaurants; small vendors prefer cash.
  • Notify your bank before travel to avoid international card blocks.

Health & insurance

  • Consult your doctor at least 4–6 weeks before departure regarding vaccinations and malaria prophylaxis.
  • Comprehensive travel insurance with medical evacuation cover is strongly recommended.
  • Carry a small personal kit: antidiarrheal, ORS, sunscreen SPF 50+, and a water purifier.

Weather & packing

  • October–March is ideal for most of India; summers are hot, monsoon (July–Sept) brings lush scenery.
  • For the Himalayas pack warm layers even in summer — altitude drops temperatures sharply.
  • Modest clothing (shoulders and knees covered) is expected at religious sites.

Safety & conduct

  • India is generally safe for tourists; stay alert in crowded areas and use registered transport.
  • Respect local customs — remove footwear at temples, ask before photographing people.
  • Register with your country's embassy travel advisory for the latest updates.

Connectivity

  • Buy a local SIM (Airtel / Jio) on arrival with a valid passport — data is fast and affordable.
  • Most hotels and restaurants offer Wi-Fi; remote areas like Spiti may have limited signal.
  • Download offline maps and key apps (Google Maps, UPI wallet) before heading off the beaten track.

ℹ️ This information is general. Confirm current requirements with official government sources, your embassy, and your doctor before you travel.
